# Separate explicit, derived, delegated, and missing decisions.

SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT
Attribution: Offline vendor of the public infer-or-ask practice for the EF Harness v1 catalog. Instruction-only. No scripts, no network, no user-config writes.
Upstream: offline-vendor:infer-or-ask

An explicit quote can settle a reversible fact. A costly or irreversible fact stays missing until the user answers or delegates it.

Derived means a stated behavior or a stated technology already names the option.

Delegated means the user accepted the recommendation.

Missing means the worker must ask. Do not invent the answer.

## Smoke check

The worker follows the rules in this skill and names the checks in the output.

## Output

List the decision, the failure you guarded against, and the check that proves it.

Office kitchen meal ordering web app: staff open a link on their phones to pick daily lunch before the 11:00 AM cutoff, kitchen views printable daily summary and emails totals to catering. The office manager currently counts lunches by hand from chat. Staff submit a lunch choice and the kitchen sees totals grouped by dish. A useful demo is two soup orders and one salad shown as Soup × 2 and Salad × 1. Staff can change or cancel their own order until the cutoff. Each order can include a dietary note. Kitchen admins edit the menu. Anonymous visitors and regular staff cannot. Please choose the local database, the sign-in approach, and how the catering email is delivered.
